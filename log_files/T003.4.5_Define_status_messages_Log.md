### Design Patterns

1. **Ellipsis Convention**: In-progress states end with "..." (Cargando..., Guardando...)
2. **Feminine Gender**: Invoice states use feminine form (Cargada, Extraída) to agree with "Factura"
3. **Present Participle**: Active states use gerund form (Cargando, Guardando)
4. **Past Participle**: Completed states use past participle (Cargada, Extraída, Conectado)
