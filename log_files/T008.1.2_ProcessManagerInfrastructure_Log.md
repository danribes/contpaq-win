## Architecture Decisions

1. **Event Emitter Pattern**: Used Map<Set> for O(1) add/remove of listeners
2. **Immutable Config**: `getConfig()` returns a copy to prevent mutation
3. **Uptime Calculation**: Calculated on-demand in checkHealth() rather than storing
4. **Path Resolution**: Uses Electron's `app.isPackaged` to distinguish dev/prod modes
